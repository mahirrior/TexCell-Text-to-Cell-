# TexCell-Text-to-Cell-
TexCell converts raw WhatsApp business chats into clean Excel sheets instantly. Paste messy text like 'Sharma Traders Cement 25 bags @420 Pending' &amp; our AI auto-identifies Party, Material, Qty, Rate, Total &amp; Payment Status into perfect columns &amp; rows. Saves 90% data entry time.
 an intelligent application built for Indian traders, suppliers, and small businesses who manage their entire business on WhatsApp.The Real Problem:
Every day, thousands of orders are received on WhatsApp in raw, unstructured text like Sharma Traders Cement 25 bags @420 Pending or Gupta - 500kg saria bhej do. Staff spends 2-3 hours daily manually typing this messy chat into Excel registers for billing and accounting. This process is slow, has typing errors, and makes tracking pending payments very difficult.Our Solution:
Chat2Excel automates this entire process. Just paste your raw WhatsApp chat or upload your exported chat .txt file, and our intelligent parsing engine converts it into a clean, structured Excel sheet in seconds.The application doesn't just copy-paste. It intelligently understands the text.1. Auto-Identify: Using NLP and pattern recognition, it automatically detects and extracts key entities from any format - Party Name, Date, Material Name, Quantity, Unit (Bags, Kg, Truck), Rate, Total Amount, and Payment Status (Paid/Pending). It works even with Hinglish, spelling mistakes, and different writing styles.2. Auto-Scale & Clean: It normalizes all data. For example, it understands 25 bags, 25 bg, and 25 Bag are the same, calculates missing totals if only Qty and Rate are given, and standardizes date formats.3. Smart Column Mapping & One-Click Excel: It automatically creates perfect columns and rows in a proper .xlsx format, ready to be directly fed into Excel, Tally, Busy, or Google Sheets. It can process 1000+ messages at once.Chat2Excel is built for the hardware, cement, steel, building material, and wholesale markets of India. It saves 90% of data entry time, eliminates errors, and gives business owners a clear ledger of pending payments, party-wise. From raw WhatsApp text to final Excel - fully automated.
